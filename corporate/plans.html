Pricing is set per served site. Enter the badged headcount at the location we deliver to and the matching tier is selected for you.
Billable headcount = badged employees at the served location — not total company headcount. A 4,000-person company with one 220-person Boston office is a Tier 3 site. This keeps pricing tied to the population we actually feed, and lets a national company start at a site-level price without a national-level negotiation.
Employees pay nothing toward the subscription. It is the corporation's access fee to the platform, the restaurant rotation and the dispatch service. Billing runs on three invoices a month — subscription on the 1st, delivery for days 1–15 on the 15th, delivery for days 16–30 on the 30th.
