Terms & Conditions

These Terms govern your use of the Office Grubb restaurant platform. By accepting orders through Office Grubb, you agree to prepare and package food to the standards described in your onboarding, to honor confirmed pickup times, and to keep your menu and availability accurate.

Office Grubb charges a permanent 10% platform fee plus a performance-based commission on gross food sales, and a 20% commission on Live Kitchen event revenue. Fees are itemized on every payout statement. Payouts are made by direct deposit on the 15th of each month for the prior month's earnings.

Either party may end this agreement with written notice. Outstanding balances remain payable. Full terms are available on request.

Privacy Policy

We collect the information you provide to run your restaurant on Office Grubb — business details, menu, order and payout records — and use it to operate the service, process payments, and calculate performance and commission.

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the partners needed to deliver orders and process payouts. You can request a copy or deletion of your data at any time.
